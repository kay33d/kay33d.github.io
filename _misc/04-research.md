### Thesis: An Empirical Investigation of Tokenization and Forgetting in Bangla OCR
Apr 2025 – Apr 2026 · supervised by Dr. Maruf Ahmed, Associate Professor, Dept. of EEE, BUET
{: .entry-meta}

- Developed and evaluated PATHOK, a TrOCR-based Bangla handwritten text recognition system, comparing ViT and CNN encoders with character, grapheme, and BPE-based tokenization.
- Investigated writer-level machine unlearning using writer-disjoint splits and prune-then-unlearn methods, evaluated through membership inference, linear probing, and retain-replay relearning attacks.
