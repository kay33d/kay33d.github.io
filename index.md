# About me

Hi, I'm Kaidul. I just finished my B.Sc. in Electrical and Electronic Engineering at the [Bangladesh University of Engineering and Technology (BUET)](https://www.buet.ac.bd/){:target="_blank" rel="noopener"} in Dhaka, and I now teach Computer Programming as an [Adjunct Lecturer at Manarat International University](misc.html#teaching).
{: .lede}

I work on computer vision, NLP and deep learning. My thesis looked at [tokenization and forgetting in Bangla OCR](misc.html#research), and I co-authored [CAE-Net](publications.html#cae-net), a deepfake image detector published in JVCIR. Lately I've been building an [LLM router](projects.html#hybrid-router) and a [knee MRI classifier](projects.html#knee-mri) for edge devices.

Outside the lab I care about teaching and access to education — I co-founded the [Thakurgaon Science Society](misc.html#leadership), which brings hands-on science to schools in remote parts of Bangladesh.

Have a look at my [publications](publications.html), [projects](projects.html), [CV](cv.html), or [everything else](misc.html). If you want to talk, [email me](mailto:kaidul.tkg@gmail.com).
