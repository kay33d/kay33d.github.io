About me

Hi, I'm Kaidul. I just finished my B.Sc. in Electrical and Electronic Engineering at the Bangladesh University of Engineering and Technology (BUET) in Dhaka, and I now teach Computer Programming as an Adjunct Lecturer at Manarat International University.

I work on computer vision, NLP and deep learning. My thesis looked at tokenization and forgetting in Bangla OCR, and I co-authored CAE-Net, a deepfake image detector published in JVCIR. Lately I've been building an LLM router and a knee MRI classifier for edge devices.

Outside the lab I care about teaching and access to education — I co-founded the Thakurgaon Science Society, which brings hands-on science to schools in remote parts of Bangladesh.

Have a look at my publications, projects, CV, or everything else. If you want to talk, email me.
