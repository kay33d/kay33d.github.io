A programmable signage system on an 8×8 dot-matrix LED display driven by an LP-2900 CPLD, showing scrolling text and animations chosen with switches.

- Modular Verilog design for display patterns, four-direction scrolling and frame animations (e.g. a walking figure), verified in Quartus simulation before hardware.
- Smooth scrolling, clean frame transitions and immediate response to mode switches on the final board.
